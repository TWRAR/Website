Using this site

This site is provided as-is, for information about TWRAR. Don't attempt to disrupt it, scrape it abusively, or use it to distribute malware.

Using TWRAR

TWRAR drives a real browser to click, fill, and scrape pages on your behalf, based on a config you write or record. Only point it at sites and content you own or otherwise have explicit authorization to automate. Do not use it against third-party sites without permission - that's on you, not on us.
