Privacy Policy

What TWRAR and this website collect (almost nothing) and why.

Last updated: 2026-09-10

The Engine (the desktop app)

TWRAR's Engine runs entirely on your own machine. It does not phone home, collect analytics, create an account, or send your recorded steps, YAML profiles, scrape results, or reports anywhere. Any credentials referenced in a config via ${env:VAR_NAME} stay on your machine, read from your own environment variables.

Local settings (browser channel, disclaimer acknowledgment, last config path) are stored in a per-user app-data folder on your machine, not shared with anyone.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent) as part of hosting it - we don't have access to that beyond what GitHub itself provides, and we don't add any analytics, trackers, or ad scripts of our own.

The only thing this site stores in your browser is a single localStorage key remembering your light/dark theme preference - see the Cookies Policy.
