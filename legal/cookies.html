This site sets no cookies, and has no tracking or advertising scripts of any kind.

What it does use

A single localStorage entry, twrar-theme, remembering whether you last chose light or dark mode. It stays in your browser, is never sent to a server, and isn't shared across sites.

The Engine (the desktop app)

Not a browser cookie at all, but worth noting here: the desktop app stores its own local settings (browser channel, disclaimer acknowledgment, last config path) in a per-user app-data folder on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere.
